# Session 2 · Geometry optimisation and harmonic frequencies in continuum solvent

*Jaguar calculations on Q and QH₂; comparison with the experimental IR spectrum*

*In preparation.*

Geometry optimisation and frequency calculations of Q and QH₂ in implicit water (PBF) at three levels of theory (PBE/LACVP\*\*++ for fast tests, B3LYP/def2-TZVPD and B3LYP-D3/def2-TZVPD), and how to read the Jaguar output: convergence, imaginary frequencies, zero-point energy and Gibbs energy. A gas-phase frequency calculation is compared with the experimental IR spectrum.
